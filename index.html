Case File 001 · Flag Hunt

Fragment Forge

Three exhibits. Three hiding places. One complete flag.

Open

Detective Brief

A trophy flag was seized and deliberately shattered into three fragments before it could be surrendered. The pieces were pinned to this bureau, but nobody wrote down where.

None of the pieces are printed on any card. Examine what each exhibit loads, then forge the three pieces back into a single flag.

Chain of custody: the exhibit hall, the field lab rig and the stylesheet were all signed out by the same clerk. What a clerk writes is not always what a page shows.
  • 01 ? Stylesheet

    The look of a page is written in a file you never see rendered.

  • 02 ? Scripts

    The rig keeps a running log that the page never prints.

  • 03 ? The Browser

    Every visit leaves a receipt behind.

Detective hint
Read the file behind the paint, read the log behind the page, and read the receipt the browser kept. Then obey the order the pieces ask for, not the order you found them in.
